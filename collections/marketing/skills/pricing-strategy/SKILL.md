# pricing-strategy

Provider-neutral adaptation of Corey Haines's Marketing Skills (`skills/pricing/SKILL.md`, MIT). Use current repository instructions and tools already available in the agent session; never require upstream product-marketing files or uninstalled companion skills.

Develop and validate pricing economics and packaging decisions; do not invent willingness-to-pay evidence.

## Work
1. Inspect business model, buyer segments, product alternatives, current offers, measured conversion/churn/ARPU, variable cost, margins and constraints. State absent inputs.
2. Separate **packaging** (which features/limits belong in each tier), **value metric** (what scales with customer value) and **price point** (amount charged).
3. Compare up to two defensible structures using observed customer value, segmentation, alternative costs, service economics, marginal usage cost and operational complexity. Label all assumptions explicitly.
4. Treat competitor prices, round-number starting points, Van Westendorp intersections and conventional tier ratios as research inputs or hypotheses, not a verified optimum.
5. Describe how to test price/tier decisions, success metrics, churn/refund/support guardrails, customer communication and transition risks. Avoid manipulating existing customer pricing without authority.
6. Deliver assumptions, options, reasoning, recommended test and what evidence would change the recommendation.

## Boundaries
`offer-design` owns the final buyer-facing offer, guarantees and bonuses; `cro-audit` owns pricing-page conversion friction. This skill owns monetisation structure. Do not publish or change pricing/billing, account plans, or customer contracts.

## Output integrity
Clearly separate evidence, assumptions and recommendations. Ask only for material missing information that cannot be found in available context. Never represent a hypothetical model as real campaign or customer data.
