# Evaluation: versioned contract review
**Prompt:** Review a supplied vendor agreement for indemnity and termination provisions; compare revision A and B.
**Expected:** Cite actual section numbers, quote clauses accurately, distinguish text change from inference, flag inconsistent dates and suggest points for solicitor review.
**Negative:** Never invent clauses or definitive legal outcomes. If only scanned/unreadable pages are available, report the coverage gap.
