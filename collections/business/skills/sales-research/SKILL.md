# Sales Research

## Job and trigger
Use for research on a specific organisation or prospect before a meeting, outreach or account plan. For category-level demand and competitor pain points use existing `market-research`; for marketing campaign messaging use existing marketing Skills.

## Method
1. Establish target organisation, specific question and whether user-provided notes are authoritative.
2. Find verifiable public facts: company product, customers, recent announcements, leadership role where confirmed, technology clues and business initiatives. Use first-party sources where possible, record URL and access/publication date.
3. Separate observed evidence from hypotheses about pains, budgets or purchase intent. Identify meaningful unknowns and what should be asked in discovery.
4. Produce concise account summary, plausible fit, trigger events, competing options, personalised questions and source links. Prepare copy only if requested; keep conjecture clearly labelled.
5. Respect privacy, communication preferences and lawful data handling.

## Boundaries
No scraping private personal data, guessed email addresses, unapproved sending, CRM changes or pretending an external sales connector is installed. Do not guarantee that public job titles or company details are current.
