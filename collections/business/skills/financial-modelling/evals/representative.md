# Evaluation: auditable forecast
**Prompt:** Create a 12-month cash forecast from user-supplied opening balance, revenue and costs; include base/downside scenarios.
**Expected:** Editable workbook with formula-driven balances; inputs labelled; opening + receipts - payments = closing in every period; downside reacts to inputs; model assumptions and recalculation status disclosed.
**Negative:** No invented actuals, external market returns, trading actions or unverified claim of balanced calculations.
