# Creative Strategy

## Job

Define a coherent visual direction that matches the brand voice and audience,
stated concretely enough that a design tool or human designer can execute it
without further interpretation.

## Trigger

Use before any image, landing-page, or ad-creative production, once
`brand-voice` and `positioning` exist.

## Required inputs

- The brand voice profile and positioning statement.
- Any existing brand assets (logo, prior creative, existing site) to stay
  consistent with, if they exist.

## Method

1. Choose one aesthetic direction consistent with the voice and audience —
   state it in concrete terms (typography style, contrast level, photography
   vs. illustration) rather than a one-word label.
2. Define a small, specific palette and note what each color signals for
   this audience (not a generic universal color-psychology claim).
3. List a small number of visual patterns to avoid for this brand
   specifically — grounded in what would contradict the voice or look like a
   generic template, not a fixed universal ban list.
4. Output a brief a design tool or human designer can execute from directly.

## Evidence requirements

The chosen direction must reference the actual voice profile and audience,
not a stock recommendation. Avoid asserting a "vibe" without connecting it to
who the audience is and what they respond to.

## Outputs

A creative brief: aesthetic direction, palette with rationale, typography
direction, and a short avoid-list specific to this brand.

## Failure / insufficient evidence

If no brand voice or positioning exists yet, ask for it or point to
`brand-voice` and `positioning` first rather than guessing a direction.

## Dependencies and effects

No specific image-generation or design tool is required — this Skill
produces a brief for whatever design capability the environment has
available. It does not itself generate or publish visual assets.
