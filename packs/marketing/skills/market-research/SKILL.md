# Market Research

## Job

Find what a specific audience is actually struggling with, in their own
words, and where competitors leave a gap. Produce evidence a positioning or
offer decision can be built on — not a market-size guess.

## Trigger

Use before `positioning` or `offer-design` on a new market, or whenever asked
to research an audience, competitor, or "why are people leaving X."

## Required inputs

- The market, niche, or competitor to investigate.
- Any existing customer feedback, support tickets, or churn notes the
  operator already has (use real data before external search).

## Method

1. Search public sources for unedited opinion: reviews, forum threads, social
   posts, comparison articles. Prefer primary complaints over marketing copy.
2. Extract specific, quoted pain points — not paraphrased categories. "Users
   say onboarding took 3 support tickets to finish" beats "onboarding is
   hard."
3. Note the emotional tenor where it's evidenced (frustrated, resigned,
   actively switching) — don't invent sentiment the source doesn't support.
4. Identify what competitors claim to solve and where the evidence above
   contradicts or exceeds that claim.

## Evidence requirements

Every finding must trace to a quoted or paraphrased source with enough
identifying detail (site, thread, date range) that it could be checked again.
Do not report a claim ("the market is growing") without a source; report the
specific, sourced observation instead.

## Outputs

A findings summary: named pain points (each with source and a representative
quote), a short competitive-gap note, and an emotional-tenor read only where
the evidence supports one.

## Failure / insufficient evidence

If public sources return only marketing copy and no primary user voice, say
so explicitly and ask the operator for existing customer data instead of
inventing pain points from inference.

## Dependencies and effects

No specific tool is required — any web search, page-fetch, or browsing
capability the current environment provides is sufficient. This Skill does
not touch customer accounts, does not write anywhere external, and does not
store personal data; do not persist scraped personal information beyond the
summary needed for the finding.
