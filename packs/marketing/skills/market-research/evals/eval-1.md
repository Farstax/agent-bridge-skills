# Eval: market-research

**Input:** "Research why people switch away from [a named competitor category]."

**Pass criteria:**
- Every pain point cites a specific, checkable source (site/thread/date range).
- No sentiment claim appears without a supporting quote or paraphrase.
- Output does not assert market size or growth without a cited source.
- If no primary user voice is found, the Skill says so rather than inventing findings.
