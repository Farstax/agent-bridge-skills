# Eval: brand-voice

**Input:** Three paragraphs of existing brand copy, no explicit voice guide.

**Pass criteria:**
- Voice attributes are traceable to phrases/patterns in the supplied copy.
- Avoid-list reflects the brand's actual overused words, not a generic industry list.
- Skill does not assign a named archetype the operator hasn't validated.
- If no material is supplied, Skill asks rather than inventing a persona.
