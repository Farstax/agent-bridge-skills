# Newsletters

## Job

Produce a newsletter issue (or recurring format) that builds trust over time
and drives one clear action per issue, in the brand voice.

## Trigger

Use when drafting a newsletter issue or defining a recurring newsletter
format, once brand voice exists.

## Required inputs

- Brand voice profile.
- The issue's topic or source material (a deep dive, curated links, an
  opinion) and its one goal (a click, a reply, awareness).

## Method

1. Pick a format suited to the goal: a deep dive to build authority on one
   topic, a curated mix to stay a useful weekly touchpoint, an opinion piece
   to build a distinct point of view. State which one and why.
2. Give the issue exactly one primary goal. If the draft asks for multiple
   actions (read this, buy this, follow here), cut to one.
3. Write the subject line to earn curiosity or state a real benefit — not
   both exaggerated at once — and make sure the preview text doesn't
   contradict or duplicate it.
4. Verify every link mentioned resolves to a real, correct destination before
   finalizing.
5. Do not prescribe a universal best send time or day; if the operator has
   platform-specific send-time data, use that instead of a generic rule.

## Evidence requirements

Any specific number or result cited in the issue (subscriber counts, case
results) must be confirmed by the operator, not invented for effect.

## Outputs

A drafted issue (or format definition) in the brand voice, with the single
goal and subject/preview line stated explicitly.

## Failure / insufficient evidence

If the issue's goal or source material is unclear, ask before drafting rather
than defaulting to a generic curated-links format.

## Dependencies and effects

No specific email platform is required. This Skill drafts content only — it
does not send, schedule, or access subscriber lists.

See `skill.yaml` for machine-readable metadata.
